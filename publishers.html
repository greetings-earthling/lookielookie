For publishers

A screen-free road trip game for the whole car.

Lookie Lookie is a cooperative spotting game for ages 3 and up. Flip a card and everyone hunts for a vehicle in that colour, or a wildcard like a bridge, a tow truck or a dog. Each card also carries an optional conversation question to talk about while you look. It works for one passenger or a full van, on a five minute errand or a ten hour drive, and the driver never has to touch the cards.

The deck

Colour cards, wildcards and conversation starters.

The full deck mixes single colours, "this or that" colour pairs for harder finds, and wildcards like a cat or dog, a bridge, the letter Z on a sign, and a summer/winter RV or snow plow card.

The designer

Made by a dad who loves playing games with his kids.

Lookie Lookie started as a game I played with my kids in the car: call out a colour and everyone looks for it. It worked so well that I turned it into a deck. It's been playtested on family road trips, and I've had a first run of prototypes printed.

I'm Dennis Houle, a marketing and graphic designer in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario. I designed the game, the cards and the art myself, and I'm looking for a publisher who can bring it to families in Canada and the U.S.
