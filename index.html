How It Works

1

Flip a card.

See what everyone is looking for.

2

Look outside.

3

Keep talking.

Made for the road

Easy for little kids. Fun for grown-ups too.

Ages 3+

1+ Players

Play alone, split the deck or get the whole car involved.

Screen-Free

Something fun to do while actually looking out the window.

No Pressure

Can't find it? Flip another card. There are no road trip referees.
