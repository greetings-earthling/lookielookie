Lookie Lookie

A Colourful Road Trip Game

A simple, screen-free game for short drives, long road trips, and everyone in between.

How It Works

Easy to Play

1. Flip a card.

See what everyone is looking for.

2. Look outside.

Spot the colour, vehicle, animal or roadside find.

3. Keep talking.

Use the optional question while you look.

Made for the Road

Ages 3+. One player or the whole car. No setup, no scorekeeping, and no pressure. Can't find it? Flip another card and keep going.
